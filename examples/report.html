Effective Sample Size
Measures sampling efficiency by calculating ESS for key parameters. Low ESS indicates high autocorrelation and poor convergence. ESS should be at least 10% of total samples.
Derived Parameter Consistency
Verifies that derived parameters match calculations from sampled parameters (e.g., total_mass = mass_1 + mass_2). Inconsistencies suggest post-processing errors or file corruption.

Network SNR Consistency
